FactFocus Labs

News agencies sit on archives of verified, trusted reporting. FactFocus helps them turn that content into new products for their clients, without leaving their own facts behind.

What it does

01 Tailored articles Clients ask in plain language and get answers written from the agency's own reporting, with sources cited.
02 Multilingual output The same verified content, delivered in the languages each client works in.
03 Micro-newswires Topic-based feeds built on the fly, so each client follows exactly the stories they need.
