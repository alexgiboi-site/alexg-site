HOW THIS WAS MADE

Compiled with Claude from each agency's own website and the member directories of the European Alliance of News Agencies (EANA) and the Alliance of News Agencies in South-East Europe (ABNA-SE), then checked by me. Only institutional contacts are listed: general and newsroom email addresses and switchboard numbers, never personal ones. Each entry links to its source and shows when it was last checked.
